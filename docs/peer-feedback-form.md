Without a verbal explanation, try to make the city calm, gently active and strongly active. Then stop input. Record the first attempt before explaining the controls. A mouse-mode test must not be entered as a microphone test.

2. Could you find and understand the interaction without help?
3. Were gentle and strong states visibly different? Which changes did you notice first?
4. Did the return to calm feel smooth or distracting?
5. Did background sounds trigger it? What was audible at that moment?
6. Did the landmarks read as Sydney? What led you to that interpretation?
7. What is the single most useful improvement?
