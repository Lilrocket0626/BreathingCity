## APA 7 patterns — examples of format, not real sources

For an accessible course slide deck:

> Author, A. A. (Year). *Actual title* [PowerPoint slides]. Learning-management-system name. URL

For a published assigned reading, cite the original publication and DOI/URL rather than inventing a course-specific publication. For a retrievable course video, record its author/date/title/platform and use the real timestamp when quoting. If readers cannot retrieve an item, determine with the course's referencing guidance whether it is a personal communication; personal communications are cited in text and do not go in the reference list.

Before counting an item, open it and note which actual argument, technique or criticism is being used. In the reflection, connect each citation to a concrete change or observation; a list of five titles alone does not demonstrate engagement.
