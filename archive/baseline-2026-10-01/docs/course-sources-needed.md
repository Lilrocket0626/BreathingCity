# Coursework Sources Needed for the Final Reflection

The A2 guidance requires at least five relevant sources from the course. Technical web references alone do not satisfy this requirement.

Upload or record the details of at least five relevant lecture materials, readings, viewings, code conundrums, or seminar resources from Canvas. Useful themes may include:

- code literacy;
- creative coding and computational thinking;
- data as material;
- iteration and debugging;
- abstraction, functions, or object-oriented programming;
- interaction design;
- ethics and authorship; and
- reflective practice.

For each source, record:

1. Author or lecturer
2. Year
3. Title
4. Resource type
5. Canvas URL or publication details
6. The specific concept relevant to Breathing City

Do not invent course sources. The final reflection cannot meet the HD synthesis criterion until the real course materials are supplied and read.
